Study Tracker – Project Statement
1. Problem Statement

Students often have multiple subjects and study tasks to manage. It can become difficult to keep track of completed and pending tasks. This project provides a simple Python-based study tracker to help students monitor their study tasks and progress.

2. Scope of the Project

The project focuses on managing study tasks through a simple command-line application. Users can enter their tasks, mark their completion status, view completed and pending tasks, and check their overall progress.

3. Target Users

The main target users are:

Students
School and college learners
Anyone who wants a simple way to track study tasks
4. High-Level Features
Adding study tasks
Tracking completed tasks
Identifying pending tasks
Calculating progress percentage
Displaying study progress through the terminal
